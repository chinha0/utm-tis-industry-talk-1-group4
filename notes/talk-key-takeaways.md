## 1. The Core Question After the Demo
- Writing working code or building a functional demo is only the starting point[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).

## 2. Technology and Information System (TIS) in Practice (MyREDS)
MyREDS (Resident Easy Decision System) connects residents, visitors, security guards, and management through a resident app, guard app, and web admin dashboard[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span)[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span):
- People: Residents and guards — requires training and support[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).
- Hardware: Phones and gate devices — requires installation and upkeep[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).
- Software: Apps and dashboard — requires reliability and updates[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span).
- Data: Access logs and reports — requires useful records and privacy[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span).
- Process: Register, verify, record — requires consistent operations[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).

## 3. The Visitor Journey (Five Handoffs)
1. Resident: Creates an invitation[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span).
2. Visitor: Receives a QR pass or link[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span).
3. Guard: Verifies access[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span).
4. Gate operation: Allows, rejects, or escalates[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span).
5. Management: Reviews the record[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span).

## 4. Users vs. Buyers & Developer vs. CEO Shifts
- Users vs. Buyers: Users (residents, visitors, guards) care about quick tasks, clear entry instructions, and dependable decisions[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span). Buyers (management) care about control, traceability, security, ROI, budget, and renewal[span_26](start_span)[span_26](end_span)[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span).
- Developer vs. CEO: Developers focus on whether the system works (behavior, performance/integration, testing, maintainability)[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span). CEOs focus on sustaining delivery (customer value and payment, people and operating costs, post-launch commitments)[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span)[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span).
- Business Model & Pilots: Revenue streams can include subscriptions, implementation, hardware, and optional services[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span). Pilots test task success, time and errors, support effort, and renewal intent[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span). After go-live, commitments continue across support, updates, incidents, and billing/renewal[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span).
